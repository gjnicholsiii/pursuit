# Accelerated Build Plan

## Objective

Usable pilot in approximately six weeks; commercial hardening immediately after pilot validation.

## Week 1 — Foundation
- Finalize company selling profile and readiness model.
- Implement opportunity + document + requirement + evidence schema.
- Connect SAM.gov opportunity ingestion.
- Select/license initial SLED breadth provider while direct connectors are built.

## Week 2 — Opportunity ingestion
- Normalize federal + SLED opportunities.
- Acquire solicitation packages and attachments.
- Search/filter/save/watch workflows.
- Source-health monitoring.

## Week 3 — Five-Minute Brief
- Extract deadlines, prebid, bonding, insurance, set-asides, certifications, award method, contract term, submission path and required forms.
- Attach source evidence to every critical fact.
- Detect missing referenced attachments.

## Week 4 — Differentiators
- Ready for Government eligibility engine.
- Path to Award procurement explanations.
- Confidence scoring based on package completeness, source agreement and extraction certainty.
- Explain exactly why confidence is reduced.

## Week 5 — Operational use
- Amendment/change monitoring.
- Explain what changed, not simply that an addendum exists.
- Pursue / Watch / Walk pipeline controlled by the customer.
- Deadline and action tracking.

## Week 6 — Pilot
- 5–10 small/midsize businesses.
- Use live opportunities only.
- Measure factual accuracy, document completeness, eligibility accuracy, comprehension and time saved.
- Human-review high-impact extraction failures and feed corrections back into rules/tests.

## Non-negotiables
- No fabricated procurement facts.
- Unknown stays unknown.
- Confidence is information confidence, never a promise of winning.
- Original source evidence remains one click away.
